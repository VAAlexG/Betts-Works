# Betts Works production launch checklist

The software MVP is complete only as a technical implementation. Public launch is blocked until all required business approvals and credentials below are supplied and recorded.

## Identity and licensing — blocking

- [ ] Final approved transparent logo file and usage rules.
- [ ] Correct legal entity name and registered business name.
- [ ] ABN.
- [ ] Motor dealer licence number, jurisdiction and licence-holder name.
- [ ] Approved dealership address or service-area wording.
- [ ] Sales phone, sales email and privacy contact.
- [ ] Trading hours and any approved response-time commitment.
- [ ] Domain name and authorised DNS administrator.

## Commercial model — blocking

- [ ] Written Betts Works/SCD Direct relationship wording approved by both parties.
- [ ] Permission to use the SCD Direct name and any supplied materials.
- [ ] Confirm whether every advertised vehicle is physically held by Betts Works.
- [ ] Confirm whether available-soon vehicles may be advertised and what evidence is required.
- [ ] Define new, used, demonstrator and imported-used classifications.
- [ ] Define pricing basis and on-road cost treatment.
- [ ] Approve warranty, statutory warranty and cooling-off wording where applicable.
- [ ] Confirm no manufacturer-authorised-dealer or manufacturer-backed-warranty claim is implied.

## Inventory and media — blocking

- [x] Current SCD Direct inventory captured and seeded for Betts Works.
- [x] Vehicle details and source photography supplied for implementation.
- [ ] Verified price, status, VIN visibility, compliance, registration and location for every listing.
- [ ] Verified engine, power, torque and towing figures where shown.
- [ ] Authorised photography of each actual listed vehicle.
- [ ] Permission to use manufacturer names/badges in listing context.
- [ ] All fictional sample rows removed from production.

## Legal and privacy — blocking

- [ ] Privacy policy approved by qualified Australian counsel/business adviser.
- [ ] Website terms approved.
- [ ] Dealer disclosure and licence placement approved.
- [ ] Consumer guarantee wording reviewed; no exclusion of non-excludable rights.
- [ ] Data processors, retention periods and cross-border disclosure reviewed.
- [ ] Analytics provider and consent approach approved, or analytics remains disabled.

## Technical launch — blocking

- [ ] Production D1 and R2 resources provisioned and backed up.
- [ ] Admin allowlist configured and tested with allowed/denied accounts.
- [ ] Email domain verified; SPF, DKIM and DMARC checked.
- [ ] Enquiry notification and acknowledgement tested; failure path verified.
- [ ] Optional Turnstile keys configured and tested, or omission explicitly accepted.
- [ ] Production migration applied and restore drill completed.
- [ ] Type check, lint, tests and production build pass on the release commit.
- [ ] Desktop, tablet and mobile journeys verified.
- [ ] Keyboard, screen-reader labels, focus states and reduced motion checked.
- [ ] Canonical URL, sitemap, robots, structured data and social preview verified on the live hostname.
- [ ] Broken-link and form-recipient checks complete.
- [ ] Stakeholder approval recorded before public access.
